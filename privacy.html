Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit this portfolio website (the "site"). I keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who is responsible

The site is run by AAYAN ("I", "me"). For any privacy question or request, write to aayan369.dev@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Contact form. If you use the form, I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to reply to you.
  • Email. If you write to me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you send.
  • Analytics (optional). Only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, I may load a privacy-focused analytics tool that counts visits and which sections are viewed. If you decline, no analytics script is loaded.
  • Your cookie choice. Your Accept or Decline choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the banner does not keep appearing. It stays on your device.

Third-party services

The site loads some files from other providers. Like any website request, this lets them see your IP address and browser details:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and keeps ordinary server logs.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) delivers the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr deliver the animation libraries.
  • Form provider and analytics tool, only if I enable them. They process data on my behalf and only as described above.

Cookies and local storage

Without your consent, the site sets no tracking cookies. It stores two small items in your browser: your cookie choice and a timestamp that stops the contact form from being used twice within a minute. You can change your cookie choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

How long I keep data

Messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a reasonable record of the conversation, and then deleted on request. Analytics data, if enabled, is kept in aggregated form by the analytics provider.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU and UK under GDPR) you may ask me to show, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, or to withdraw your consent. Email me and I will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page.
